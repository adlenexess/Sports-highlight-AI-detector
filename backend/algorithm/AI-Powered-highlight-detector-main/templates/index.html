How it works

  1. Motion detection — measures frame-to-frame movement to spot fast action.
  2. Audio analysis — detects crowd roars and commentary spikes.
  3. Scene-cut detection — broadcast camera cuts cluster around big moments.
  4. Replay detection — flags broadcaster slow-motion replays as bonus evidence.
  5. Fusion & ranking — all four signals are combined into one excitement score, and the top moments are stitched into highlight.mp4.
